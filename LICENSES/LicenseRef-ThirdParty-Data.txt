Third-party source data and derived output

The covered file is either a third-party dataset redistributed as it was
acquired, or an output derived from such a dataset. Libre AI grants no licence
over that material. The applicable copyright, database and reuse terms are those
of the producer identified in the accompanying copyright notice, as published by
that producer.

The provenance record required before publication — stable identifier, source
and acquisition channel, original licence, transformations, EU sui generis
database rights analysis, and personal-data classification — is not complete for
this material. Until it is, no first-party outbound licence is asserted over it
and none may be inferred from the other declarations in this repository.

What is unresolved is the reuse licence of the source dataset, not the
confidentiality of its content. This material records votes cast publicly by
elected representatives in the exercise of their mandate, and their publicity is
a legal obligation; the marker asserts no restriction of its own on that basis.
The open question is which upstream terms govern redistribution and derivation —
the repository's own documents still leave that choice open.
